The second thing worth recording today came from helping my wife play Gems of War. On the surface, she was stuck, her team setup looked messy, and resources were upgraded in a strange order. I could not help smiling: some common cards were pushed to level 13, while stronger epic cards were still at level 1, and the lineup had little structure.

But the interesting part was that she was genuinely enjoying the game. That moment reminded me that games are not only about getting stronger, clearing faster, or maximizing efficiency. Exploration and engagement can be meaningful by themselves.

Thinking a bit deeper, what is truly worrying is not being bad at a game or not knowing something yet, but slowly losing curiosity with age and losing interest in everything. In the AI era, the knowledge barrier is getting lower and lower. As long as we are willing to learn, there are treasures to discover in both work and play. The most important thing is to enjoy the process in whatever we do.
