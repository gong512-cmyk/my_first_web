Today was a rewarding day for building my AI assistant ecosystem.

This morning I used OpenClaw to systematically organize my work logs and meeting notes. Following defined rules, I consolidated the data into a "Top 5" framework and process management structure. This marks the first step of bringing AI into my work management system. Going forward, the weekly Top 5 generated by OpenClaw will become a key input for my decision-making. The process wasn't without friction — plenty of issues came up — but the theoretical foundation is clear: building a knowledge base and a system, maintained consistently. I'm looking forward to what it can bring.

This evening I made an important call: I signed up for GitHub Copilot's $10 plan and connected it to Hermes Agent. Zhipu's GLM-5.1 is genuinely strong, but the throttling has pushed me past my limit. I've now settled on a dual-plan setup: OpenCodeGo (great for mainland China access) plus GitHub Copilot (backed by Microsoft's stability). This combo covers my own daily use and leaves headroom for my wife's Hermes Agent too.

But the most electrifying moment today happened in a meeting.

The top boss asked me to give an overview of the factory's recent process discipline inspections. He had heard that management was slipping, and he wanted a summary — including data, facts, process, and results — in one minute. Before AI, this kind of surprise question would have been nerve-wracking.

I opened Obsidian. Drawing from the process discipline inspection records I had been steadily accumulating in my knowledge base, I assembled a structured report in under a minute. Data, facts, process, results — all of it. The top boss was convinced on the spot.

This was the first time I truly felt the power of AI-augmented work in live combat. Not a gimmick. Real competitive advantage. All those quiet hours of knowledge base maintenance paid off in a single, crucial minute of calm. That kind of edge is hard for anyone else to replicate.

Three directions going forward: one, vibe coding. Two, web development. Three, deepening the second brain. All three are clear. Now it's about patience and depth.
