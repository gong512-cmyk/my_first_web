Today marks a real milestone in knowledge management. Inspired by Andrej Karpathy's public LLM Wiki architecture—what he calls the "Zettelkasten + LLM" model—I finally gave my personal knowledge base the overhaul it needed.

Karpathy's framework is beautifully simple: a Source layer for raw materials, a Wiki layer for compiled knowledge, and a Schema layer for structural guidance. Information lands, gets digested, then becomes structured, searchable, updatable knowledge. It is not about dumping files into a folder. It is about making the LLM an active knowledge steward.

I did three concrete things today. First, I split my Factory and Personal Obsidian vaults into completely separate entities, each with its own three-layer architecture. The logic domains are too different to coexist—mixing them only creates noise. Second, for the build toolchain, I used Claude Code for the main framework (ran out of quota quickly) and then switched to China's GLM-5 to continue. I have to say, domestic models can hold their own in this kind of scenario now. Third, I unified all directory paths across the system for consistency.

The core goal is one thing: a truly functional personal second brain. Taking scattered knowledge fragments and systematically extracting them into a continuously updatable, efficiently searchable network. In this AI era, the most valuable thing is not public knowledge on the surface—it is your unique insights, the perspectives shaped by your own values. Writing down what you think and feel is the work that actually matters.

But building is only the start. The real challenge ahead is the maintenance mechanism—how to consistently feed valuable information into the knowledge base after analysis, keeping the snowball rolling. There is also an even more exciting direction: the personal profile. I have already started letting the system accumulate data about my values, personality, and interests. It is rudimentary right now, but the long-term vision is to let this data generate judgments and assistance aligned with my character. Moving from "storing materials" to "compiling knowledge"—this road has only just begun.

## Key actions
- Restructured personal knowledge base architecture using Karpathy's three-layer LLM Wiki theory
- Separated Factory and Personal Obsidian vaults, each with its own three-layer structure
- Used Claude Code + GLM-5 in relay to complete the architecture build
- Unified and updated all directory paths
