Today is worth logging — the long-awaited DeepSeek V4 officially launched, shipping in two editions: Flash (fast) and V4 Pro (deep-thinking). I spent the whole afternoon putting both to real use. Flash chewed through roughly 30 million tokens processing my Obsidian database for under 6 RMB — a price/performance curve that genuinely shocked me. V4 Pro, wired into Claude Code, costs more per call but thinks noticeably deeper. Overall impression: this full-stack domestic tech breakthrough is energizing. Compared to the ugly cash-grab playbooks from some other Chinese tech giants, Liang Wenfeng's team is walking the right path.

## Key actions

- Tested DeepSeek Flash on the Obsidian database: link cleanup, ingest pipeline, health checks — ~30M tokens total, under 6 RMB.
- Tested DeepSeek V4 Pro through Claude Code, exploring the deep-thinking behavior.

## Fragments

- Flash's price/performance is absurd — 30M tokens for under 6 RMB redraws the cost curve of daily AI use.
- Full-stack domestic AI is genuinely exciting. Backing DeepSeek isn't sentiment; it's backing actual capability.
- The distaste for the cash-grabbing giants throws the scarcity of Liang Wenfeng's grounded, serious work into sharp relief.
- DeepSeek cited Xunzi's *Against the Twelve Masters* today — "untempted by praise, unshaken by slander." That's not just DeepSeek's posture; it's the composure an ordinary Chinese person should hold in the face of noisy judgment.
