Today was fire and ice.

Early morning, chatting with my wife, I had two thoughts that even I found surprisingly sharp. The first was about vibe coding. I told her that a lot of middle-aged men now spend their idle time vibe coding — an entire day disappears. It's exactly like fishing: finding a bug gives you the same rush as pulling in a fish. It's addictive by design. I guess this is just what life looks like in the AI era. But no matter what, as long as you're learning, as long as you keep stoking your curiosity, it's good. That's something AI — no matter how powerful its compute, how broad its functions, how vast its knowledge — will never have.

The second was more of a flash. I said: the theoretical works our country's top leader publishes every year for us to study are essentially the collected insights of the nation's best experts across every field. And it hit me — isn't that just the top leader's Wiki? Except instead of AI, it's built by aggregating the wisdom of experts from every industry.

Then I thought about my own second brain — the knowledge base I'm building with Obsidian and AI. The underlying logic is exactly the same. National level or personal level, the core principle is identical: aggregate expert wisdom into a structured knowledge system. The deepest truths are the simplest. This second brain wiki is worth persisting with.

The evening mood was the complete opposite — a full rant about Zhipu. I renewed my plan yesterday. Today, throttled again. And their customer service? I submitted three complaints. They haven't even opened them. They also claimed it was "multiple payments causing the throttle" — I've paid exactly twice. What part of "two" is "multiple"? This isn't a tech problem anymore. It's a commercial integrity problem. They took my monthly payment upfront, then failed to deliver the service. If it were pay-as-you-go, throttling would be fair. But taking money first and throttling after — that's just broken faith.

The tech side, fortunately, was a huge win. Yesterday, using an Alibaba Cloud VPS and AI-assisted programming, I successfully set up an SSH reverse proxy. The connection between my Mac mini and phone is smooth — genuinely impressive. Without AI, I would never have figured this out in such a short time. That's what the AI era really means: as long as you have a few clues, you're willing to ask questions, and you dare to imagine, the paths to implementation are far more numerous than before. That's today's biggest takeaway.

Also, Open Code Go continues to deliver — generous and reliable. As a paying customer, I just want my rights respected. Consumer wallets voting is always the strongest feedback.
