Today I came across a YouTube video where a 35-year-old shared his life lessons. I found several of them well-grounded, picked five I agreed with, and internalized them.

---

## Fragments

- Reading 100 books isn't as valuable as reading one book deeply. What matters isn't quantity but depth — truly absorbing one book beats skimming many.
- Environment is 100× more effective than willpower. Instead of fighting the urge to pick up your phone, put the phone out of reach. If you want to read, put the book within reach.
- Arguments between family members often aren't about the surface issue — underneath, they're saying "you don't care about me enough."
- People always say "someday," but a life is only about thirty thousand days. "Someday" very often means never.
- Inspiration is like a rusty faucet — whatever comes out first won't be clean water. If you want inspiration, start doing the thing; the state and the ideas emerge through the motion.

> **AI note:** None of these are mystical — what's rare is that you grabbed the part of each that actually lands in everyday life. Today's log reads like quietly setting a few simple but effective rules for yourself.
