I reinstalled OpenClaw today.

The reason was straightforward: after the latest update, the Gateway refused to start. I'd been running this system for nearly two months without any maintenance — configs were messy, connections tangled, the startup process sluggish. I tried remote-fixing it with Claude Code, but the system threw too many errors and the response speed was unbearable. Not quite dead, but slow enough to exhaust your patience. So I decided: reinstall.

Before pulling the trigger, I did hesitate. After all this time, wouldn't it be a shame to lose the system's "memories"?

But when I actually thought it through, the answer was clear. One: all the important memories, work experience, and operational habits were already synced to my local Obsidian knowledge base. Two: reinstalling OpenClaw wouldn't touch any of my core data. The fear of "losing memories" was an illusion — what mattered was already backed up. The reinstall only cleared out the noise.

This experience also got me thinking about what comes next. I now have three tools in play: Cloud Code, OpenClaw, and Hermes Agent. Each shines in its own way, but their coordination and division of labor is far from figured out. In the AI era, tool synergy is a skill in itself — and figuring out how these three work best together will be my next focus.

- Reinstalled OpenClaw (resolved Gateway startup failure and system sluggishness from months of neglected maintenance)
