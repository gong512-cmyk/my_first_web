This is the first time the "vibe coding from anywhere" scenario fully clicked, the payoff from all those earlier hours building the VPS reverse-connection and port-forwarding infrastructure.

Second: I used prompt engineering to generate a VPS infographic. The result was better than expected — aside from some Chinese font rendering issues on the model's side, the layout and composition were commercial-grade, ready to ship without further edits.
