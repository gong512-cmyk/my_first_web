Today I took my own website live for the first time using vibe coding.

It sounds simple in one sentence. It wasn't. I hit every trap along the way — wrong configs, broken paths, services not starting, ports not listening — each one had to be dug out and fixed. But in the end, the site is accessible on the VPS. The URL works: http://47.100.103.66.

It's a strange feeling. I've watched other people build websites before and thought, "yeah, I get it." But doing it yourself from scratch, you realize how wide the gap is between understanding and executing. Today, I crossed that gap — from "I can see how it's done" to "I did it with my own hands." That leap alone is worth marking.

But honestly, a working site is only step one. The harder stuff is still a fog: how do I integrate GitHub version control into this workflow? How do I maintain and iterate on features going forward? How do I get the ops pipeline straightened out? These pieces are still blank spaces.

So today isn't the finish line. It's the real beginning — I've officially entered the hands-on phase of vibe coding and web operations. Plenty left to learn, plenty left to build. But at least the door is open now.
